Feature Explanations

Age is a significant risk factor for heart disease. Risk increases with age, particularly after 45 for men and 55 for women.

Males generally have a higher risk of heart disease than females, especially before age 55. After menopause, women's risk increases.

Chest pain types range from typical angina (classic heart-related chest pain) to non-anginal pain. Asymptomatic patients may still have heart disease without experiencing chest pain.

  • Typical Angina (0): Classical heart-related chest pain
  • Atypical Angina (1): Not all classical features of angina
  • Non-Anginal Pain (2): Not related to heart
  • Asymptomatic (3): No symptoms

Resting blood pressure above 140/90 mmHg is considered hypertension, a major risk factor for heart disease.

  • Normal: Less than 120 mmHg
  • Elevated: 120-129 mmHg
  • Stage 1 Hypertension: 130-139 mmHg
  • Stage 2 Hypertension: 140 mmHg or higher

Total serum cholesterol levels above 200 mg/dL increase risk of heart disease.

  • Desirable: Less than 200 mg/dL
  • Borderline High: 200-239 mg/dL
  • High: 240 mg/dL and above
